When the Nord Stream pipeline ruptured in September 2022, satellite data detected the leak before official confirmation. TROPOMI measured methane plumes reaching 40 km downstream, Sentinel-2 captured surface disturbances, and Sentinel-1 SAR showed coherence loss. The satellites saw what happened hours before inspection crews could reach the remote location.

This tutorial covers satellite leak detection: Databricks + PySpark + Sedona architecture processing terabytes of TROPOMI methane, Sentinel-2 NDVI, and Sentinel-1 SAR coherence data at continental scale, multi-sensor composite anomaly scoring combining physical signatures, and medallion structure (Bronze/Silver/Gold) delivering daily leak scores with explainable detection.

This matters because pipeline operators spend billions on inline inspection and aerial surveys, but 95% of infrastructure goes unmonitored on any given day. A 100,000 km midstream network would require 274 helicopters flying every day to achieve weekly coverage. Satellites image the entire system daily, regardless of terrain, weather, or access restrictions.
